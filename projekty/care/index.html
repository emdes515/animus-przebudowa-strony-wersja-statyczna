CARE: Climate Action RPG

English Title: CARE: Climate Action Role-Playing Experience

Autorska gra symulacyjna i fabularna poświęcona negocjacjom w obliczu kryzysu ekologicznego. Projekt obejmował kurs trenerski w Zagrzebiu, serię rozgrywek w szkołach, wydanie polskiej edycji gry oraz prezentację na Kongresie Edukacji Doświadczalnej 2025.

Merytoryczne Założenia i Cele Inicjatywy

Dossier Merytoryczne

Autorska gra symulacyjna i fabularna poświęcona negocjacjom w obliczu kryzysu ekologicznego. Projekt obejmował kurs trenerski w Zagrzebiu, serię rozgrywek w szkołach, wydanie polskiej edycji gry oraz prezentację na Kongresie Edukacji Doświadczalnej 2025.

language International Project Abstract (English)

Proprietary experiential role-playing game modeling systemic multi-stakeholder decisions under climate crisis scenarios. Included training in Zagreb, classroom simulations, release of the Polish print edition, and showcase at the Experiential Learning Congress 2025.

CARE Board Game: Fostering Climate Awareness

🎲On 30 October, Animus Foundation organized next local session as part of the CARE: Climate Action Roleplay Experience project, showcasing the CARE board game, which aims to raise awareness about climate change and its effects on our environment. 🌍We’re thrilled by the positive responses we continue to receive, as well as the enthusiasm the game sparks among participants!🫶🌳In this engaging role-play, participants work together to save a forest, facing decisions that influence the ecosystem for better or worse. This hands-on experience brings a new understanding of our individual and collective roles in environmental protection.💪A huge thank you to everyone who joined us today! Your energy and interest make this journey toward climate action all the more meaningful. 🌱Project co-funded by the European Union 🇪🇺

CARE: Training Course in Zagreb

From June 8th to 14th, our team participating in a training course in Zagreb, Croatia 🇭🇷, as part of the CARE project. ☘️Our partners, Interaktiva, are the hosting organization, and ASNU – Associazione Scienze Naturali Unite is the leading organization. 🤝During this training course, participants learn about the creation of educational games, explore the causes and consequences of climate change, and delve into the complexities of this topic. 🎲 They also learn how to conduct debriefings and understand their importance, as well as how to facilitate educational games.👀
